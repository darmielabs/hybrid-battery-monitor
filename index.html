Hybrid Battery Monitor

Check the hybrid battery of your 2004–2009 Toyota Prius block by block, with a cheap Bluetooth OBD2 adapter and your Android phone.

Coming soon to Google Play · free, no ads

What you see

All 14 blocks

Live voltage of every battery block and the spread between the highest and lowest, at rest and under load.

Resistance per block

Internal resistance calculated over the whole trip, so a weakening block stands out early.

Trends

Compare trips over weeks and months: spread, resistance, allowed power, inverter temperature and fuel use.

Battery health

State of charge, current, temperatures, allowed charge and discharge power, and a cooling check.

Engine

Fuel trims, coolant, catalytic converter check, readiness monitors and fault codes.

Trip report

Distance, estimated fuel use, share driven electric and why the engine was running.

How it works

  1. Plug a BLE OBD2 adapter into the port under the steering wheel (tested with Veepeak OBDCheck BLE).
  2. Pair it once in the app.
  3. Drive. When the car goes to READY, the app starts recording by itself, also with the screen off. When you switch off, the trip report is ready.

No adapter yet? The app has a demo mode with a simulated car.

Read-only and private

  • The app only reads. It cannot clear fault codes or write anything to the car.
  • No internet permission: your data stays on your phone. Export trips as CSV whenever you want.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Supported cars

Toyota Prius 2nd generation (NHW20, 2004–2009). Beta, not yet tested on a real car: Prius 2009–2015 (ZVW30), Lexus CT 200h, Auris Hybrid, Prius v / Prius+, Prius c / Aqua / Yaris Hybrid (2011–2020). Needs a Bluetooth Low Energy OBD2 adapter; classic Bluetooth adapters do not work. Android 12 or newer. Available in 25 languages.
