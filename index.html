Hybrid Battery Monitor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

Hybrid Battery Monitor does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet access: it does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What the app does

The app connects via Bluetooth Low Energy to an OBD2 adapter (for example a Veepeak OBDCheck BLE) in your car and reads diagnostic values from the car (hybrid battery block voltages, temperatures, engine data, fault codes). It only reads; it never writes to the car or clears fault codes.

Data stored on your phone

Trip recordings (CSV files), trip reports and the Bluetooth address of the paired adapter are stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. They stay on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Export and sharing

Only when you tap “Export” does the app copy your trip files to the Downloads/PriusMonitor folder on your phone. Only when you tap “Share” does Android's share menu open with the report text; you choose where it goes. The app itself never sends anything.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to find and connect to your OBD2 adapter. Not used for location.
  • Notifications: to show that a trip is being recorded and when a trip report is ready.
  • Foreground service (connected device): to keep recording while the screen is off during a drive.
  • Start at boot: to keep watching for your paired adapter after a phone restart.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
